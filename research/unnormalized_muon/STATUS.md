Optimizer change (opt-in NANOCHAT_UNNORMALIZED_MUON=1): keep native momentum/Nesterov. Let G be the tensor entering NS, before row equilibration. Compute U with the existing row equilibration, five Polar Express iterations, and Frobenius renormalization. Compute one scalar per matrix s=max(0,sum(G.float()*U.float())). Preserve native NorMuon state and transform, then multiply its direction by s, before applying the existing cautious decoupled decay. Neither scalar/vector/embedding AdamW groups nor weight decay are multiplied by s. Applying s after the norm-preserving transform is essential; an earlier insertion would be normalized away.

For exact U=polar(G), s is the nuclear norm of G, dual to the spectral norm. Finite NS, row equilibration, and downstream NorMuon make this a practical adaptation, not an exact LMO. No claim of a dual norm of the raw minibatch gradient: G is native Nesterov input. No normalization/averaging over matrix elements or stacked matrices; no upper clipping.
